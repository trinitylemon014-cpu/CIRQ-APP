Community Guidelines

CIRQ is for students. These rules keep it kind and safe.

Be kind

  • No bullying, harassment, hate or threats.
  • Don't impersonate other people or schools.
  • Respect when someone blocks you.

Keep it safe

  • Don't share phone numbers, home addresses or other private details.
  • Meet at school events and public places. Tell a trusted adult where you're going.
  • No sexual, violent or illegal content.

Be real

  • No spam, fake accounts or buying followers. Follower ranks are measured from real follows.
  • Verified badges are given only after a real review.

Reports and consequences

Use the Report option on any post, comment, profile, message, story or event. Moderators can remove content, suspend accounts and keep records of their decisions.

Paid features

Boosts and paid trivia entries (when available) are paid manually by mobile money. Students under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission. Boosts never change CIRQ STATUS ranks.
