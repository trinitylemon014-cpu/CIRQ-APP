Privacy Notice

What we collect

  • Account details: username, name, school, email (private, never shown to others).
  • What you post: posts, reels, stories, comments and messages.
  • Basic usage needed to run CIRQ (likes, follows, game and trivia results, and which reels you watch for a few seconds, which the reel's owner can see).
  • If you use paid features: the mobile-money number and account name you register, and payments an admin records. Only administrators can see these.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect precise location.
  • We don't sell your data or show ads.
  • Administrators never see your password.

Your controls

  • Make your profile followers-only in Settings.
  • Block and report anyone.
  • Delete your account in Settings. Your posts, stories, messages and uploaded files are removed.

Media

Photos and videos are stored with our media provider (Cloudinary). Chat attachments use private signed links. Stories are deleted after 24 hours.

Retention

Expired stories and reset links are deleted automatically. Moderation and audit records are kept to protect students.
