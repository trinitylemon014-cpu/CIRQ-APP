{{ m.icon('help','i-sm') }} How lending works
  • Gifts are instant and final. Up to {{ cfg.GIFT_MAX_COINS }} coins at a time.
  • Loans need both of you. The lender picks the amount ({{ cfg.LOAN_MIN_COINS }}–{{ cfg.LOAN_MAX_COINS }}), a due date and a late fee. The borrower reads the agreement and taps “I agree”. Coins move only then, and an unanswered offer lapses after {{ cfg.LOAN_OFFER_HOURS }} hours.
  • On the due date (end of the day, Kampala time) CIRQ automatically takes the coins from the borrower and pays the lender. Reminders go out 3 days before, 1 day before and on the day.
  • If the borrower is short, CIRQ takes what they have. A one-time late fee (the lender's %, up to {{ cfg.LOAN_MAX_FEE_PCT }}%) is added to the unpaid part only, and {{ cfg.LOAN_GARNISH_PCT }}% of every coin they earn goes to the lender until it is paid.
  • Borrowers can pay back early. Lenders can forgive a debt at any time. Never lend what you can't afford to lose, and report anyone who pressures you.
