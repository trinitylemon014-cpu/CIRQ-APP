How it works

  1. Register the mobile-money number you'll pay from and the name that shows when you send money.
  2. An admin verifies it (usually within a day).
  3. Send the money to {% if config.PAYMENT_NUMBER_DISPLAY %}{{ config.PAYMENT_NUMBER_DISPLAY }}{% if config.PAYMENT_ACCOUNT_NAME %} ({{ config.PAYMENT_ACCOUNT_NAME }}){% endif %}{% else %}the number shown by CIRQ admins{% endif %} from your verified number.
  4. When the admin sees your payment it's credited to your account: a trivia entry or a boost. Nothing is automatic and nothing is taken from your phone by CIRQ.
